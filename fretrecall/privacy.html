Fret Recall — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026

In short: Fret Recall does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. Your settings and practice stats stay on your device. We have no servers, no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking.

1. Data we collect

We do not collect any data. Fret Recall has no user accounts, no backend servers, no analytics, and no advertising or tracking. The app does not connect to the internet, and we never receive your information.

2. Data stored on your device

The app stores the following only on your device:

  • Your app settings (instrument, practice mode, tempo, key, sound level and similar choices)
  • Practice progress, such as your daily goal count and which notes you find hardest

This data never leaves your device. Deleting the app removes it.

3. Device permissions

The app may ask for the permissions below. Each is used only on your device for the stated purpose, and granting it does not send any data to us:

  • Microphone — in Listen mode, to hear the notes you play on your bass or guitar. The sound is analyzed on your device as you play, and is never recorded, stored, or sent anywhere.
  • Bluetooth — to connect wireless MIDI instruments and page-turner foot pedals.

4. Third-party services

Fret Recall uses no third-party services, analytics, or advertising SDKs.

5. Children’s privacy

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

6. Data retention and deletion

Because your data is stored only on your device, you remain in control of it. Deleting the app removes its data from your device. You can also reset your practice stats from the app’s settings at any time.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the effective date above.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact us at fretrecallfeedback@teetimemusic.com.
