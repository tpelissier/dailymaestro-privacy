Fretboard trainer for bass and guitar, on iPhone & iPad

Need help? Email fretrecallfeedback@teetimemusic.com with your question, your device model, and your iOS version, and we’ll get back to you.

Getting started

  • Pick your instrument: 4-string bass, 5-string bass, or guitar.
  • Pick a practice mode with the mode button: Practice (find every place a note lives), Tempo (find each called note on the beat), By String (focus on the strings you choose), Arpeggio (play every tone of a called chord), or Chords (see and hear any chord’s shapes up the neck).
  • Pick how you answer: tap the neck, play into the microphone, or play a MIDI instrument.
  • Tap Start and play.

Common questions

How does the microphone mode work?

Choose Listen as the input and play your bass or guitar near the device (or through an audio interface). Fret Recall hears the pitch you play and checks it. The sound is analyzed on your device and is never recorded or sent anywhere. Allow microphone access when asked.

Can I use a MIDI instrument or a foot pedal?

Yes. Connect a MIDI instrument over USB or Bluetooth and choose MIDI as the input. A Bluetooth page-turner foot pedal can start, pause and step the drills hands-free; turn it on in the trainer’s settings.

Can I practise only the notes in one key?

Yes. In the trainer’s settings, turn on “Only notes in a key” and choose the key. The note drills then call only that key’s notes, spelled the way that key is read.

Where is my data stored?

Everything stays on your device. There are no accounts and no servers. See our Privacy Policy for details. Deleting the app removes its data, and you can reset your practice stats in the trainer’s settings.

What are the requirements?

Fret Recall requires iOS / iPadOS 17.6 or later.
